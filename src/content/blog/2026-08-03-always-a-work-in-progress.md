I never did get my danny brown dotcom domain, my name is too common and I was too late to the game. For years, I had a dotcom that I didn't really like, and I underutilized it, in part because it was a version of my name that I never really *use*, so it felt weird to link and share.

I was doing some domain hygiene recently and noticed the `.dev` domain option. *I'm a dev*, I thought, *I wonder if plain dannybrown dot dev is available*? If you're a discerning reader, you will have already figured out that it was indeed available and I did indeed buy it.

Now, being a dev, it's like, a double-edged sword. On one hand, I'm a dev! `dannybrown.dev` is perfectly advertising that right in its name. But on the other hand, I'm a *dev*, and a `.dev` environment kind of strongly implies a development environment, i.e., not production. So there's a part of me that's a little dissatisfied, like the website hasn't *shipped*, you know?

But then, I realized: *I* am always in development. I'm most productive when I'm learning new things, figuring out more efficient ways to do repeat things, and just generally leveling up. If I ever call *myself* done and shipped to prod, that's kind of the day I've given up – something I don't intend to do. With that in mind, the double-edged sword morphs into a dual positive meaning that makes me pretty darn stoked with my new domain.
